Evaluation framework

We define:
\[ \mathcal{S} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot \sigma(\hat{y}_i, y_i), \quad \left(\sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i = 1\right) \]
\[ \sigma = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{Correct} \\ 0 & \text{Missing} \\ -1 & \text{Incorrect} \end{cases} \]

Discrete Fields: Exact matching.

Free-text Fields: Levenshtein dist. (threshold of 0.85).

Utility: \( \mathcal{S} \in [-1, 1] \), where positive values indicate added value.
