Evaluation

Extraction Score

We evaluate pipeline performance using a weighted utility score defined as
\[ \mathcal{S} = \sum_{i=1}^{32} w_i \cdot \sigma(\hat{y}_i, y_i), \quad \left(\sum_{i=1}^{32} w_i = 1\right) \]
\[ \small \sigma = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{Correct} \\ 0 & \text{Missing} \\ -1 & \text{Incorrect} \end{cases} \]

Numeric/Dates...: Exact matching criteria.

Text: Levenshtein dist. (threshold $\ge 0.85$).

Score range: \( \mathcal{S} \in [-1, 1] \)

Extraction Confidence

Confidence is modeled based on answer modifications and retries across the pipeline
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Conf} = {} & \text{Conf}_{\text{OCR}} \times \text{Conf}_{\text{Span}} \\ & \times \text{Canon. Type} \times (0.85)^{N_{\text{teacher retry}}} \end{aligned} \]

OCR / Span: DocTR logprobs and mismatch metrics.

Canonicalization Type: Penalizes formatting changes.

Teacher Retries ($N$): Penalty for every revision.
