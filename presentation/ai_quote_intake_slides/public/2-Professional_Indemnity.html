3. Coverages & Extension

Insuring clause & extensions
8.1 Core Professional Indemnity Coverage

Standard cover typically includes: (a) civil liability for negligent act, error, omission or breach of professional duty in the conduct of professional services; (b) unintentional misrepresentation or misstatement; (c) defamation arising from professional services; and (d) defence costs and expenses incurred with the insurer's consent, on a claims-made basis, subject to the territorial and jurisdictional limits and retroactive date stated in the schedule.

8.2 Optional Extensions

Tick the extensions required and complete sub-limits / deductibles where applicable. Final terms are subject to underwriting approval.

8.4 Key Standard Exclusions (summary)

Typically excluded (subject to full wording): (a) bodily injury / property damage (except where arising from professional services and specifically covered); (b) fines, penalties and punitive damages; (c) deliberate, fraudulent or criminal acts of the insured (once established by final adjudication); (d) known claims or circumstances prior to inception; (e) trading losses or guarantees of financial performance; (f) employers' liability and motor / aircraft / marine liabilities (other than advice-related exposures).
